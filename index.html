01 / UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

A changing climate affects everyone.

Climate change describes long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns. These changes can happen naturally, but human activities are the main cause of the warming observed since the Industrial Revolution.

The Asia-Pacific is home to diverse environments, from large cities and agricultural regions to coastal communities and small Pacific islands. Understanding climate change helps communities prepare for its effects.

02 / REGIONAL IMPACTS

What is changing around us?

Climate change can affect natural environments, communities, and everyday life.

🌡️
CLIMATE

Rising Temperatures

Increasing temperatures can contribute to heatwaves and place stress on people, crops, and ecosystems.

🌊
OCEANS

Rising Sea Levels

Sea-level rise can increase coastal flooding, erosion, and risks to low-lying communities.

🌧️
WEATHER

Changing Rainfall

Changes in rainfall patterns can contribute to floods in some areas and drought conditions in others.
